## 주요 색상 (From spartaclub.kr analysis)

### Primary Colors
- **Sparta Red**: #E8344E (메인 브랜드 컬러 - CTA 버튼, 강조 요소)
- **Dark Gray/Black**: #1E1E1E (헤더, 푸터, 텍스트)
- **White**: #FFFFFF (배경, 텍스트)

### Neutral Colors

## Typography
- **Primary Font**: Pretendard Bold (한글 웹폰트)
- **Fallback**: sans-serif

## Accessibility Notes
- Main red (#E8344E) on white background: ~3.8:1 contrast ratio
- Black (#1E1E1E) on white: 18:1 contrast ratio ✓ WCAG AAA
- White on red: ~3.8:1 (use for large text only)

## Usage Guidelines
- Use Sparta Red for CTAs and important actions
- Dark gray/black for body text
- Light backgrounds for readability
- Ensure sufficient contrast for accessibility
